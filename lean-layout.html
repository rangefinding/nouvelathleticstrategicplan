Mission

We will create disciples and cultivate champions. Disciples of Christ and champions of character, effort, and leadership.

Carried under the mission of the school: called by Christ to learn, love, and lead… courageously.

Vision

To be recognized as a leading Catholic school athletic program that nurtures the physical, mental, and spiritual growth of all participants, producing outstanding athletes and responsible citizens.

To learnOur athletic programs strive to provide valuable life lessons.
To loveBuild confidence in our student-athletes to love oneself and service to others, by loving others and placing others before oneself.
To leadOur coaches and student-athletes demonstrate exemplary leadership and excellence in competition, the classroom, and the community.
CourageouslyWe live our mission and exemplify our faith with conviction and courage.

Three commitments

These govern every pillar. Where the plan and a commitment disagree, the commitment wins.

  1. We begin and end in prayer. Every practice, contest, meeting, roundtable, seminar, banquet, and council session, without exception.
  2. We show up for the program next door. No sport at Nouvel succeeds alone. Coaches make other coaches better, athletes fill other teams' bleachers, and every program leaves the ones around it stronger.
  3. We measure what we say matters. Every outcome in the plan carries a number, an owner, and a date.

Program goals

  • Measure our success by how well individuals and teams live the mission.
  • Staff teams with coaches of excellent character who teach the game well and relate positively to students and adults.
  • Celebrate the joy of competition and the benefits of physical fitness.
  • Develop athletes and teams that keep improving, work hard, and know the fundamentals.
  • Allocate equipment and resources equitably for participants of each gender at all skill levels.
  • Provide a safe environment for all athletic activities.
  • Produce teams that are competitive and strive to win.
  • Offer a variety of team sports and encourage a breadth of athletic experiences.
  • Embrace and expect failure as part of competing, training, and practice.
  • Foster a culture of commitment and school pride.
  • Hold unwavering respect for opponents, officials, and the rules of competition.
  • Develop an appreciation for the traditions, rules, and cultures of each sport.
  • Build healthy, supportive team environments based on trust, with no tolerance for hazing.

Our namesake

On December 3, 1675, Father Henri Nouvel, S.J., a Jesuit missionary, celebrated the first Catholic Mass in Michigan's interior, on Ojibway Island in Saginaw. He traveled in winter, far from home, to bring the faith to people who had not yet heard it.

That is what courageously looks like. Every Nouvel athlete carries his name onto the field.
