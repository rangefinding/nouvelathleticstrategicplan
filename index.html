Mission

We will create disciples and cultivate champions. Disciples of Christ and champions of character, effort, and leadership.

Carried under the mission of the school: called by Christ to learn, love, and lead… courageously.

Vision

To be recognized as a leading Catholic school athletic program that nurtures the physical, mental, and spiritual growth of all participants, producing outstanding athletes and responsible citizens.

To learnOur athletic programs strive to provide valuable life lessons.
To loveBuild confidence in our student-athletes to love oneself and service to others, by loving others and placing others before oneself.
To leadOur coaches and student-athletes demonstrate exemplary leadership and excellence in competition, the classroom, and the community.
CourageouslyWe live our mission and exemplify our faith with conviction and courage.

Three commitments

These govern every pillar. Where the plan and a commitment disagree, the commitment wins.

One

We begin and end in prayer

Every practice. Every contest. Every meeting, roundtable, seminar, banquet, and council session. Opened in prayer and closed in prayer, without exception and without apology. This is the first thing a visitor should notice about NCC Athletics and the last thing an athlete should forget.

Two

We show up for the program next door

No sport at Nouvel succeeds alone. Coaches make other coaches better, athletes fill other teams' bleachers, and every program leaves the ones around it stronger than it found them. We ask athletes to put teammates before themselves; we hold ourselves to that same standard across sports.

Three

We measure what we say matters

Every outcome in this plan carries a number, an owner, and a date. Where we do not yet have a baseline, we say so plainly and go get it. A plan without measurement is a wish, and our student-athletes deserve better than a wish.

Guiding objectives

Create an athletic culture of excellence by unlocking the full spiritual, emotional, and athletic potential in our student-athletes.

  1. Assess the current state of Nouvel. Where do we stand? Analyze participation rates, performance records, and feedback from coaches and athletes — through student focus groups facilitated by the Athletic Director or another liaison, program director and coach one-on-ones, and surveys of parents, coaches, and athletes.
  2. Identify areas for improvement. Based on that assessment: increase participation, provide support for the Athletic Director, enhance coaching skills, improve facilities, and strengthen Nouvel's athletic culture.
  3. Develop a plan. Outline specific strategies — increase outreach to potential athletes, provide professional development for coaches, and invest in new facilities.
  4. Implement the plan. Assign responsibilities and timelines for each aspect, and communicate the plan to coaches, athletes, and other stakeholders.
  5. Monitor progress. Regularly measure against our goals and adjust as necessary, collecting data, seeking feedback, and surveying to test whether our strategies are working.
  6. Celebrate successes. When we achieve a goal or make significant progress, say so publicly. This builds momentum and motivates coaches and athletes to keep going.

Program goals

  • Measure the success of our program by the degree to which individuals and teams embody the core values of the mission statement.
  • Staff teams with coaches of excellent character, competent in teaching technical skills, who can motivate and relate positively to student-athletes and adults.
  • Celebrate the joy of competition and the benefits of physical fitness.
  • Develop athletes and teams that are constantly improving, hard-working, disciplined, and schooled in the fundamentals of their sport.
  • Allocate equipment and resources equitably and appropriately for participants of each gender at all skill levels.
  • Provide a safe environment for all athletic activities.
  • Produce teams that are competitive and strive to win.
  • Offer a variety of team sports and encourage a breadth of athletic experiences.
  • Embrace and expect failure in the face of competition during training, practice, and gameplay.
  • Foster a culture of commitment and school pride. Students on school teams are expected to prioritize that commitment over non-school activities, reflecting the dedication and responsibility central to our Catholic identity. The coach and Athletic Director collaborate on sport-specific protocols that set expectations, address conflicts, and apply the policy fairly and consistently.
  • Hold unwavering respect for opponents, officials, and the rules of competition as an absolute and non-negotiable expectation of every member of this community.
  • Develop in athletes an appreciation for the traditions, rules, and cultures of their sport.
  • Build healthy, enjoyable, supportive team environments based on trust, with no tolerance for hazing.
