Our namesake

Father Henri Nouvel, S.J.

On December 3, 1675, Father Henri Nouvel, a Jesuit missionary, celebrated the first Catholic Mass in Michigan's interior, on Ojibway Island in Saginaw. He traveled in winter, far from home, to bring the faith to people who had not yet heard it.

That is what "courageously" looks like. Every Nouvel athlete carries his name onto the field.
